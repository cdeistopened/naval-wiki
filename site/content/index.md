# *39 frameworks. Wealth, happiness, and decision-making.*

<p class="hero-subtitle">An open-source archive of Naval's philosophy on building wealth without renting your time, finding happiness through peace of mind, and making better decisions. Features 39 framework articles, interactive skills for Claude Code, and source citations to Naval's work.</p>

## How It Works

Three layers. **Frameworks** are written guides grounded in Naval's philosophy on wealth, happiness, ethics, and decision-making. **Skills** are interactive — they ask about your situation and apply frameworks step by step. **Indexes** provide thematic navigation across the archive.

Frameworks explore Naval's core ideas: why you can't get rich renting your time, the role of specific knowledge and leverage, the path to peace through truth, and the relationship between desire and unhappiness.
